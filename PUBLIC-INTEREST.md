## Public-interest contribution already established

The study provides a documented decision **not** to productize a detector whose out-of-sample accuracy, hard-finding precision, and practical first-use predictive value failed their gates. This reduces the risk of publishing an automated tool that could create false reports against open-source maintainers.

That is a research-output contribution, not proof of broad social benefit.

## Evidence ladder

The project distinguishes:

1. **Research output** — code, protocol, data, negative result, and reproducibility artifacts exist.
2. **Research dissemination** — the result is publicly described or presented.
3. **External reproduction** — an independent party runs or re-annotates the study with preserved evidence.
4. **Maintainer response** — a maintainer supplies ground truth or responds to a specific finding.
5. **Accepted correction** — an upstream correction is accepted or merged.
6. **Measured user benefit** — evidence shows reduced false reports, review effort, onboarding time, or another predeclared outcome.
7. **General social impact** — replicated effects extend beyond isolated projects and are supported by appropriate study design.

Do not collapse these levels.

## What does not count by itself

The following are activity or reach indicators, not impact outcomes on their own:

- opening an issue or pull request;
- receiving a GitHub star;
- repository views or clones;
- release downloads;
- social-media impressions;
- an AI-generated review;
- an unverified claim of adoption;
- rerunning the original agent's labels and calling it independent.

## Stronger public-interest evidence

Evidence that can support a stronger claim includes:

- independent reproduction with preserved raw evidence;
- independent human re-annotation;
- citation in research, policy, or documentation practice;
- maintainer-confirmed ground truth;
- merged upstream correction with a traceable rationale;
- another project adopting the evidence-preservation protocol;
- measured reduction in false reports or maintainer review effort;
- evidence that a productization decision changed because of the negative result.

## Reporting policy

All public-interest claims must be entered in `results/public-interest/impact-log.json` and `.csv` with a source, date, evidence level, verification status, and non-duplicated outcome. Rejected, disputed, and reversed claims remain in the log.

See `docs/impact-measurement-policy.md` for definitions and acceptance rules.
