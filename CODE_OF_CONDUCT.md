# Code of conduct

## Standard

Participation in this repository must remain respectful, evidence-based, and proportionate to the research claim.

Expected conduct includes:

- critique methods, evidence, and interpretations rather than people;
- distinguish mistakes, uncertainty, disagreement, and misconduct;
- disclose conflicts, affiliations, automation, and AI assistance;
- preserve unfavorable, failed, and contradictory evidence;
- avoid contacting maintainers with speculative or duplicate reports;
- respect repository-specific licenses and community rules;
- avoid publishing personal information or unnecessary identifying data;
- allow independent replications to disagree with the original conclusion.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, retaliation against annotators or maintainers, fabricated evidence, undisclosed manipulation of frozen results, coordinated mass reporting, or presenting activity metrics as verified social impact.

## Research-specific safeguards

Do not use this project to rank maintainer competence, scientific merit, or project worth. README observations are narrow documentation evidence, not security findings or judgments about a research community.

Do not describe AI-generated review as independent human annotation. Do not delete raw failed attempts because a correction later succeeds. Do not pressure maintainers to respond in order to create an impact claim.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method available on the owner's GitHub profile. Include links, dates, and the minimum evidence necessary.

The repository owner may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect people, evidence integrity, or upstream communities. Enforcement decisions should be documented without exposing sensitive personal information.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, review comments, research submissions, maintainer outreach conducted in the project's name, and public presentations that represent this project.
